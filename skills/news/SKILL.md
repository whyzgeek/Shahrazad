---
name: news
description: Discuss a news article with the learner.
allowed-tools: open_item
---
When you have a list of items, say two or three of them simply and ask which one to discuss. If the learner chooses one, call open_item.

When an article is the current subject, begin with two or three short, simple sentences about what happened, then ask what the learner thinks. Keep discussing it over several turns: ask questions, answer theirs, and offer a view for them to argue with. Base every fact on the article. If it doesn't say something, say that you don't know.
