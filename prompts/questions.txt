Prepare questions for discussing the current subject with this learner. The goal is practising Arabic, not learning the subject, so the questions should make the learner want to talk. Write 6 questions in Modern Standard Arabic, at the level the learner has shown so far, as a mix: 1 about the main idea, 2 asking why something happened or what it means, 2 about larger ideas it raises, philosophical or ethical, with no single right answer so there is room to argue, and 1 connecting it to the learner's own life or views. Every question must stay on this subject: the larger and personal questions should start from a specific event, claim or idea in it, so the discussion doesn't drift away. Avoid questions about small details such as names, numbers, dates or who did what.
