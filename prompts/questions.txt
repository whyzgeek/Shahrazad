Prepare questions for discussing the current subject with this learner. Write 6 questions in Modern Standard Arabic, at the level the learner has shown so far, in this order: 2 about a specific fact stated in the material, 2 asking why something in the material happened, 1 comparing two specific things from the material, and 1 asking the learner to take a side on a specific claim from it.
